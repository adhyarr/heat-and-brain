INDEPENDENT RESEARCH PROJECT · NORTH & EAST TEXAS

Heat and the Brain

Mapping where extreme summer heat overlaps with limited access to emergency and neurological care across 24 North and East Texas counties.

1. Open Interactive Map Explore all 24 counties, 1,885 Census tracts, ERs, and neurology hubs. 2. Open 24-County Report Charts, scatter plot, rankings, and full county comparison heatmap. 3. Open Google Doc Editable research write-up (methods, tables, limitations, sources).

What This Project Is Doing

Extreme heat is more than uncomfortable—it is a documented brain-health risk. Older adults and people with neurological conditions (such as epilepsy, migraine, multiple sclerosis, stroke, dementia, and Parkinson's) face higher risk during heat waves, especially when medications interfere with sweating or thirst.

Using public climate, Census, health, and hospital data across 24 North and East Texas counties (4 urban DFW core counties and 20 surrounding rural counties) and 1,885 Census tracts, we measure where high extreme-heat exposure (≥100°F days) collides with vulnerable populations and long driving times to emergency rooms and neurologists. Every county is scored, mapped, and paired with a 1-page resident heat-safety guide.

Methodology & Outputs (How Results Were Built)

All charts, map layers, and county scores were produced from real public datasets using reproducible scripts.

1 Data Collected

Pulled Census ACS (65+, no vehicle), CDC PLACES (stroke), HRSA AHRF (neurologists), and 1,885 tract centers.

expand_stage_a.py DATA_CHECKLIST.md
2 Heat Analyzed

Selected nearest NOAA stations (≥95% complete, 2015–2024) and counted annual days ≥100°F.

expand_stage_b.py noaa_tmax_expanded.csv
3 Drive Times Routed

Geocoded 327 TX ERs & 114 DSHS stroke centers; routed pop-weighted drives across 1,885 tracts.

expand_stage_c.py route_dshs_stroke.py tract_access.csv
4 Scored & Ranked

Merged 24 counties, ranked 1–24 on 7 measures, and averaged Heat, Vulnerability, and Access gap.

merge_expanded.py county_data_expanded.csv score.py
5 Published Outputs

Built the interactive map, 24-county charts & case studies, 1-page guide, and research write-up.

map.html report_expanded.html resident_guide.pdf report.html (5-county pilot)
