Heat and the Brain: Heat Exposure vs. Neurological Care Access in Rural Counties Near Dallas

HOSA project draft. Tables are blank templates. Fill them only with real, cited data.

1. Research Question

In rural counties near Dallas, which areas combine high heat exposure with low access to neurological and emergency care, and which patient groups are most at risk?

2. Hypothesis

Rural counties with more extreme-heat days and fewer clinics, neurologists, and EMS stations will have higher risk for people with neurological conditions (epilepsy, migraine, MS, stroke, dementia, Parkinson's).
