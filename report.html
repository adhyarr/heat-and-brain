What the data suggests

  • Navarro ranks highest: no neurologists on record, the highest stroke rate (3.7%), and moderate-to-high heat.
  • Wise has the longest drive to an ER (6.6 miles, about 16 minutes) and the second-most heat days among rural counties.
  • Henderson has the oldest population (22.4% age 65+) but the fewest heat days.
  • Dallas is the hottest county but has the shortest drive to an ER and 10 neurologists per 100,000.
  • All ER drives are short (2–7 miles), so neurologist access separates the counties more than ER distance does.
Read with care. This shows association, not causation. Rural neurologist counts are tiny (0–2), one weather station stands in for each county, ER distance is measured from the county center, and equal weights are a choice. Full limitations are in the written draft.
